Step 3 — secp256k1: Public Key Generation

Step 4 — Keccak-256: Ethereum Address Derivation

Step 5 — EIP-55: Checksummed Address

Apply EIP-55 mixed-case checksum encoding to the Ethereum address. The underlying 20-byte address remains unchanged.
